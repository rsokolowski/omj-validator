# Model A/B: Claude (Haiku / Sonnet / Opus 5.5) vs gemini-3.8-flash (prod) - 2026-10-12

Run with the procedure in [model-ab-playbook.md](model-ab-playbook.md). First
test of the Anthropic provider on real student photos. The owner approved this
one evaluation as data controller (prod photos sent to Anthropic before the
DPIA items for Anthropic are filled in); `ANTHROPIC_PROCESSOR_APPROVED` was set
for the eval process only, not in prod.

## Setup

- Sample: 17 completed prod submissions, 27 photos, no typed text.
  - 10 with human ground truth: the ids of the 2026-09-05 A/B (graded by hand
    then). Includes the hard cases from that run: ffa34843 (three pages of
    crossed-out work), 982d4161 (rotated, faint photo).
  - 7 recent photo-heavy submissions (2-3 photos each where available, plus two
    single photos on etap2 and a 1-point etap1): 7226f6d6 e34cbdf9 8710ac8b
    7c552067 024c2448 a358cf18 4f83ff4e. No human grade; compared against the
    stored prod score and each other.
- Same prompt for every arm (`app/ai/requests.py`), prod Gemini settings from
  `.env.prod`, Claude with `ANTHROPIC_EFFORT=high`, adaptive thinking,
  refusal fallback on Opus/Sonnet, no prompt caching. Two passes per model.
- Commit under test: `1f666b7` + `797f7fb`.

## Reading the misses

- **Haiku cannot read the handwriting.** It gives 0 to correct etap1 work and
  says so in the feedback ("zdjęcia są obrócone i częściowo nieczytelne"). It is
  not a grading-quality problem, it is image reading. Not usable for scoring.
- **Opus and Sonnet read every photo.** Their misses are judgement calls on
  legible work, not misreads: Opus pass 2 on bf1aff8c found a real flaw in the
  integrality step and gave 0 where the human gave 2; on 3a2f3f15 both gave 0
  once where the human gave 1 for partial progress. Sonnet twice gave 1 point to
  96c8f749 (a single worked example, human 0) - the more lenient direction,
  which matters more for a student.
- **Gemini's one miss** is the same ffa34843 tail case as on 2026-09-05 (3 pages
  of crossed-out work): 3 in one pass, 1 in the other.
- Ten human-graded submissions is a small sample: read the table as "Opus is
  in the same band as gemini-3.8-flash on hard photos, Sonnet slightly below,
  Haiku far below", not as a measured ranking between the top three.

## Cost and latency

Per submission, from `scoring_meta.estimated_cost` (checked by hand against
the token counts and the published prices for every arm).

| model | mean cost | max cost | median s | max s | median input tokens |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| gemini-3.8-flash | $0.034 | $0.114 | 42 | 163 | ~9.7k |
| claude-haiku-5-5 | $0.004 | $0.006 | 15 | 31 | ~23k |
| claude-sonnet-5-5 | $0.059 | $0.085 | 12 | 21 | ~23k |
| claude-opus-5-5 | $0.116 | $0.170 | 15 | 29 | ~23k |

- Gemini is priced at the promo rate valid until 2026-12-31; from 2027-01-01
  the same usage costs about twice as much (~$0.07), which is ~60% of Opus.
- Claude sees ~2.4x the input tokens of Gemini for the same PDFs and photos
  (different image/PDF tokenization), and spends far less on thinking: Gemini's
  cost is dominated by thinking tokens (up to 21k on one submission), Claude's
  by input.
- Latency is the clearest Claude advantage: 12-15 s median and no tail, against
  Gemini's 42 s median and a 163 s worst case (the 180 s timeout is close).

## Recommendation

Keep gemini-3.8-flash as the default for now. Opus 5.5 is a credible alternative
for grading photos - equal accuracy within this sample, a third of the wait,
no latency tail - at ~3.4x today's cost (~1.7x after the Gemini promo ends).
Sonnet is acceptable but leans lenient. Haiku must not be used for grading.

Before switching prod to Opus: fill in the Anthropic DPIA items, re-run this
on a larger human-graded sample (20+), and re-check after 2027-01-01 pricing.

## Cost tracking in the app

Every grading stores `provider`, `model`, tokens and `estimated_cost` in
`submissions.scoring_meta`; every extraction/hint/pattern call stores the same
in `ai_usage.meta`. Nothing aggregates or displays it yet - a weekly cost per
provider needs a SQL query today.
